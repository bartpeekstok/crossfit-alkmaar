Frequently asked questions

I'm not fit enough to start
That’s exactly why you should start. We scale every workout to your level.
I don't have time to train 5x a week
You don't have to. Many members train 2-3x a week and already see great results.
Am I too old to start training?
Absolutely not. Our average age is 35+. We have members from 20 to 70+ who train safely.
What if I have injuries or limitations?
We adjust movements or choose alternatives. Our coaches have experience with rehabilitation.
Is CrossFit suitable for beginners?
Absolutely. At CrossFit Alkmaar everyone starts with a 28 day kickstart where you learn all the basic movements in small groups (max 6 people). Every exercise is adapted to your level, so no experience is needed.
What's the difference between CrossFit and a regular gym?
At a regular gym you train alone on machines without guidance. At CrossFit Alkmaar you always train under professional coaches, in small groups with varied workouts. You get stronger, fitter and build a community.
Who is CrossFit Alkmaar for?
CrossFit Alkmaar is for everyone: from 20 to 70+, from complete beginners to experienced athletes. Our average age is 35+. Every workout is scalable to your level, goals and any limitations.
